Step 3 of 6 — How It Works

Verification is simple and secure.

Here is exactly what the system does — nothing more, nothing less.

1
Your identity has already been confirmed

You entered your Pension ID and phone number, and we matched them to your pension record. This step is complete.

2
We check you are present using your camera

The system uses your phone or computer camera to confirm you are a real, living person — not a photograph or recording. You will be asked to look at the screen, blink, or gently move your head.

3
A verification result is recorded

The outcome is sent directly to the Cross River State Pension Board. You will receive a reference number to keep for your records.

No photographs or recordings are stored. The verification check only confirms your presence and does not identify you beyond what you have already provided.

Step 4 of 6 — Preparing You

What you will do next.

There are just two actions. Both are brief.

📷
Allow camera access

When your browser or phone asks permission to use the camera, tap "Allow". This is the only time you are asked.

👁️
Look at the screen

A face guide will appear. Look directly at the screen and follow the simple on-screen prompts — blink or gently turn your head.

💡
Sit in good light

Face a window or a well-lit room. Avoid having a bright light behind you. Remove glasses only if you usually do not wear them.

⏱️
It takes under a minute

Once the camera is ready, the verification check itself takes less than 60 seconds to complete.

If verification cannot be completed

If the camera check does not work — for any reason — you will not lose your progress. Simply visit any approved support center, where a trained agent can complete the verification for you in person without a camera.

Step 6 of 6 — Camera Access

Your camera is needed for face guidance.

We use your camera only to display a live view so you can position your face correctly. No recording is made.

🙂
Position your face inside the oval

Look directly at the screen and stay still

💡
Choose good lighting Sit facing a window or bright light. Avoid sitting with a window or lamp behind you.
📐
Hold your device steady Hold your phone at arm's length or prop it upright. Keep your face centred in the oval guide.
😐
Look natural Keep a neutral expression. Remove sunglasses. It is fine to wear prescription glasses or a headscarf.

Your browser will ask for permission to use the camera. Tap "Allow" when prompted. You can revoke access at any time from your browser settings. Declining permission will not affect your pension status.
