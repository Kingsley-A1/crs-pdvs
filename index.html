Simple Process

How Verification Works

Three straightforward steps to confirm your pension status — completed in under ten minutes.

1
Enter Your Details

Provide your Pension ID and one secondary identifier — your date of birth or registered phone number. Your account will be located and your identity confirmed.

2
Complete Face Verification

Follow the on-screen guidance to complete a simple liveness check. The process is brief, calm, and designed with older users in mind.

3
Receive Your Confirmation

Your verification result is displayed immediately. A reference number is generated for your records and the Pension Board is notified.

No Smartphone? We Can Help.

If you are unable to complete verification from a personal device, qualified agents at approved centers across all 18 LGAs are ready to assist you.

Visit an Approved Center
Agents available at all LGA offices, Monday to Friday.
Call the Pension Board
Speak to a support officer who can guide you through the process.
Ask a Family Member to Help
Any trusted relative with a smartphone can help you complete verification.
